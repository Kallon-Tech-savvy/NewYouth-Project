# Open Decisions and Release Gates

This document prevents unresolved values from being silently invented during implementation.

## 1. Numerical values and thresholds still to determine

- Predefined annual investment-return percentage and its evidence/rationale.
- Minimum observation counts and geographic coverage for each category/location estimate.
- Material price-shock alert thresholds and supporting evidence requirements.
- High-impact dispute criteria and operational thresholds.
- Review deadlines for pending/unverified price submissions.
- Retention periods for raw submissions, published observations, audit logs, and contributor identity records.
- Standard appeal deadline and extension rules.
- Privileged maximum session lifetime.
- List of sensitive actions requiring reauthentication.
- Login throttling/lockout thresholds and durations.
- Exact second-warning timing shortly before the 180-day inactivity suspension.
- Evidence-based primary reference-income benchmark and its position in the observed income distribution.
- Exact emergency-reserve and savings projection calculation conventions where not yet specified.

## 2. Investment-return downside policy

The standard return policy is confirmed: one predefined annual rate, with an advanced user-defined override. The policy for negative returns and downside scenarios is unanswered. Do not assume a choice.

## 3. Retention and legal review

Create and approve a retention schedule before launch. Confirm applicable legal and privacy obligations with appropriate local expertise. Do not treat this documentation as a legal-compliance certification.

## 4. Pilot evidence

The pilot must establish whether category/location estimates meet minimum quality requirements. Until they do, show limitations or suppress affected estimates rather than manufacturing a number.

## 5. Operational readiness

Before public launch:

- Assign an owner for the public security contact.
- Define security-report monitoring, triage, and escalation.
- Define incident response and risk-based notification procedures.
- Ensure privileged accounts can enroll in and recover 2FA.
- Test authorization, session invalidation, audit logging, and data deletion/anonymization behavior.
- Confirm review and appeal staffing, including a plan for conflicts of interest.
- Verify the correction workflow and public methodology change log.

## 6. Product release safeguards

- No fabricated observations, rates, thresholds, or pilot findings.
- No claim that a feature is implemented based only on policy documentation.
- No market-wide claim from an isolated report.
- No silent changes to published results or historical methodology.
- No automatic user budget changes.
- No double-counting emergency reserves.
- No representation of hypothetical investment returns as guaranteed.
