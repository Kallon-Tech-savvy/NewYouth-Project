# TNY-017 — Security and privacy verification

- **Status:** Proposed / not started
- **Specification:** [Implementation Specification v1](../specs/implementation-spec-v1.md)
- **Dependencies:** `TNY-004`, `TNY-007`

## Objective
Verify security/privacy controls and identify operational gaps.

## Tasks
- Test least privilege, cross-user access, privileged 2FA/recovery, session revocation, inactivity, and reauthentication.
- Review logs, secret handling, throttling, and error responses.
- Verify deletion/anonymization against an approved retention schedule.
- Document security-contact ownership, monitoring, triage/escalation, and risk-based incident disclosure.
- Identify legal/privacy questions requiring qualified local review.

## Acceptance criteria
- Relevant authorization/session failures are tested.
- Secrets and credentials do not appear in logs or public responses.
- Retention/legal questions remain release gates rather than guessed values.
- A security contact is not called operational without an owner and response process.
