## Objective
Provide clear standard and advanced scenarios without taking control away from users.

## Tasks
- Expose supported income/resources, expenses, remittances, debt, savings, reserve goals, contribution timing, and inflation assumptions.
- Display deficits and quantified alternatives while preserving original inputs.
- Distinguish nominal balance, purchasing power, applied return/inflation, period, and overrides.
- Disclose fallback inflation and feasibility limitations.

- Return-dependent outputs remain gated where policy is unresolved.
- Copy does not imply guaranteed outcomes.
