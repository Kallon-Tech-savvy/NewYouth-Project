## Objective
Make corrections and methodology changes traceable and reproducible.

## Tasks
- Preserve original/corrected values, reason, actor, timestamp, and source record.
- Version calculation methodology and maintain a public changelog for consequential changes.
- Recalculate affected estimates where valid while preserving prior results.
- Label material correction notices and support risk-based containment for misleading results.

## Acceptance criteria
- Tests prove original state/history is retained.
- Corrected results are reproducible under the applicable methodology.
- Materially affected outputs are labeled corrected; no silent rewrite occurs.
- Whole-report withdrawal is used only when overall conclusions are no longer defensible.
