## Implemented schema boundaries

The provider-neutral schema increment now covers these domain persistence boundaries without implementing the application:

1. **Identity and access references:** stable internal profile/actor records and role assignments, without duplicating authentication credentials. Keep contributor identity/private profile data separate from public observation records. Exact auth-provider mapping remains implementation-dependent.
2. **Reference data:** pilot locations, price categories, item/variant definitions, and standardized units/context needed to establish comparability.
3. **Price evidence:** submissions/observations with submitter, location, category/item, price and currency, normalized quantity/unit, observed-at timestamp, category-specific context, source type, and lifecycle status. Store the actual observation time separately from record creation/update time.
4. **Review and status history:** append-only status transitions, reviewer/actor, reason, timestamp, and requested clarification. Enforce separation of duties in service/database authorization; schema constraints alone cannot encode every conflict-of-interest rule.
5. **Corrections and appeals:** immutable correction history referencing the affected record, original/corrected values or version references, reason, actor and timestamp; appeals/reconsideration linked to the original decision. Exact appeal deadlines and retention periods remain open.
6. **Publication and estimates:** separate approved/published eligibility from raw submissions; store methodology versions and estimate runs/results with source-period, counts, coverage, freshness and evidence tier. Exact minimum counts/coverage remain gated by pilot evidence.
7. **Simulator scenarios:** user-owned or anonymous/local scenarios, clearly separating observed values from user-defined assumptions; do not seed an unapproved return percentage or resolve Decision 101 in schema defaults.
8. **Audit/privacy support:** access-controlled audit events and deletion/anonymization references, without hard-coding unresolved retention durations.

Detailed schema artifacts are recorded in [domain-schema-v1.md](../architecture/domain-schema-v1.md), [`0001_domain_schema.sql`](../../db/migrations/0001_domain_schema.sql), its destructive [rollback](../../db/migrations/0001_domain_schema.down.sql), and the [SQL invariant checks](../../db/tests/0001_domain_schema_invariants.sql).
