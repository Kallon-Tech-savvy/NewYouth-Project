## Objective
Provide role-appropriate submission and review workspaces.

## Tasks
- Contributor view shows own statuses, clarification requests, and reasons where appropriate.
- Reviewer view shows assigned eligible cases and necessary evidence.
- Authorized core-team view supports escalations without broad identity exposure.
- Use in-platform notifications and audit relevant actions.

## Acceptance criteria
- UI and API role boundaries are tested.
- Contributors cannot see others' private submissions/enforcement details.
- Reviewers cannot act on conflicted or unauthorized cases.
- MVP does not require SMS/email.
